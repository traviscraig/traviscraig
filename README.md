Hey, I'm Travis

Embedded Systems Engineer | Software Developer | Maker

Trying to figure out the world one disrupted electron at a time.

I'm an electrical and software engineer with around 15 years of experience developing embedded systems and integrating hardware and software.

Professionally, I've worked on everything from avionics and ruggedized display systems to robotics and embedded computing platforms. My experience spans software architecture, firmware development, hardware development, integration, debugging, and technical leadership.

Outside of work, I'm a maker at heart. I enjoy experimenting with microcontrollers, designing electronics, building prototypes, 3D printing, woodworking, and occasionally solving problems that probably didn't need solving in the first place.

This GitHub is where I share my personal projects, engineering experiments, and the occasional questionable idea that somehow became a working prototype.

Projects & Experiments

I'm usually working on a few projects at any given time, ranging from practical engineering tools to experiments that started with "I wonder if this would work?"

Most of my personal projects revolve around embedded systems, electronics, software development, and hardware prototyping. I particularly enjoy projects that combine multiple disciplines, bringing together firmware, hardware, mechanical design, and custom user interfaces.

Some projects are intended to solve a particular problem, while others are simply opportunities to explore a new technology, learn something, or build something interesting.

I try to document the process along the way, including design decisions, challenges, testing, and lessons learned—not just the finished product.
